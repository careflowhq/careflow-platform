# Follow-Up Rules

## Appointment Reminder

The system sends a reminder:
- 24 hours before appointment
- 2 hours before appointment

## Post Consultation Follow-Up

The system sends a follow-up:
- 48 hours after consultation

## Inactive Patient Rule

A patient becomes inactive when:
- no appointment activity for 90 days

## Alert Rules

Generate HIGH alert when:
- patient reports severe symptoms
- patient misses multiple appointments

Generate MEDIUM alert when:
- no response after 3 reminders
