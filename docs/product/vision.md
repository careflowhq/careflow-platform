# Vision

## Problem

Private clinics struggle to perform continuous patient follow-up after consultations.

Assistants are overloaded with WhatsApp communication and manual reminders.

This causes:
- patient abandonment
- low treatment adherence
- operational overload
- lost revenue

## Vision

Build a multi-tenant healthcare engagement platform that automates patient follow-up workflows for chronic care management.

## Long-Term Goal

Become the infrastructure layer for patient engagement and chronic care workflows across LATAM and future US expansion.

## Initial Market

Private clinics in Peru.

## Core Features

- patient follow-up automation
- WhatsApp reminders
- patient retention workflows
- alerts and monitoring
- clinic dashboards
